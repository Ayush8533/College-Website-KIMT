About Krishna Group

Where dreams are born, inspirations are given and achievements are made

Est. 2020

Krishna Institute of Management and Technology (KIMT), established in 2020, operates under the aegis of Krishna Welfare Educational Society — a registered, non-government, non-profit educational organization engaged in training, research and consultancy across Management, Computer Applications, Para Medical Science, Engineering, Science, Arts, Commerce, Vocational Education, and more.

Our sprawling campus features lush green grounds, state-of-the-art infrastructure, modern laboratories, a well-stocked library, and excellent sports facilities. With 100+ courses available, we nurture future leaders every day.

Located at Sona on Aonla–Sirauli Road, we are well-connected to Bareilly, Aonla, Moradabad, and Delhi — easily accessible for students across the region.
